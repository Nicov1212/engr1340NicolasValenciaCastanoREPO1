In order to multiply two integers together, you simply consider x as the value you want to multiply and y as the number of groups of x, and add y groups of x.

For instance, consider the following:

`x = 3; y = 5`

This translates to "five (5) groups of three (3)", or `5*3`.

So you add 3 five times, for `3+3+3+3+3` which equals `15`.

Example:

x = 6; y = 3

`3*6 = 6+6+6 = 18`
